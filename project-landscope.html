◆ About the Project

LANDSCOPE was designed as an infrastructure intelligence system for helping municipal teams move from reactive maintenance toward evidence-based prioritization.

The platform brings together visual inspection evidence, citizen complaints, asset information, geographic context, historical maintenance records, AI-generated risk assessment, budget constraints, and crew availability.

The result is a workflow that helps identify high-risk infrastructure and determine which maintenance actions should be handled first.

◆ AI Agent Modules

01 • Vision Defect Detector

Detects infrastructure defects from inspection imagery and extracts visual evidence such as defect type, severity, and measurements.

02 • Multimodal Analyzer

Combines visual evidence with descriptions, reports, geographic context, and infrastructure information to produce a complete assessment.

03 • Historical Asset Retrieval

Retrieves previous maintenance records, interventions, and asset history to identify recurring problems and provide historical context.

04 • Risk Prioritization Planner

Uses infrastructure risk and available resources such as budget ($10K–$60K) and crew capacity to generate a prioritized maintenance queue.

◆ Technical Challenges

01 • Multimodal Analysis

Challenge: Decisions require more than image analysis.
Solution: Combine visual evidence with reports, GIS context & repair history.

02 • Resource-Aware Prioritization

Challenge: High-risk assets need prioritization with real constraints.
Solution: Include budget & crew availability in the maintenance workflow.

03 • Real-World GIS Context

Challenge: Problems are strongly connected to location.
Solution: Use Leaflet GIS complaint mapping for physical geographic context.
